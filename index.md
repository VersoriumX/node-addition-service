# VersoriumX Technology Inc

**The Future of Asset Management and Real-Time Insights.**

VersoriumX is a modular, high-performance framework designed to provide real-time tracking and analysis of both digital and physical assets. Whether you're monitoring precious metals or managing a diverse cryptocurrency portfolio, VersoriumX offers the tools you need in one unified interface.

## Key Features

### 🛠️ Modular Architecture
Our framework is built from the ground up to be modular. Easily add new data sources, custom charts, or specialized tracking modules without disrupting your core workflow.

### 📈 Real-Time Price Monitoring
Integrated with industry-leading APIs to provide live updates on:
- **Precious Metals**: Gold (XAU), Silver (XAG), Platinum (XPT), and more.
- **Industrial Metals**: Copper, Nickel, and others.
- **Cryptocurrencies**: Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and emerging tokens.

### 🔌 Web3 Integration
Seamlessly connect your Web3 wallet (MetaMask, etc.) to view and manage your on-chain assets directly within the VersoriumX dashboard.

### 🚀 Developer Friendly
Designed by engineers for engineers. VersoriumX is easy to deploy, customize, and upgrade.
